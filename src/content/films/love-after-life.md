Jack is a new employee at the Dayz company, a company which brings holidays to the world. Jack isn't taking too well to his repetitive tasks of spreading winter across the world. Suddenly he begins having a dream that triggers memories he's never had. Throughout his work day he experiences micromanagement with his boss, Mr. Time, and has bizarre run-ins with the rest of the employees, before learning what his dream is really about.
