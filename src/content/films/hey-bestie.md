Daisy, an OCD homebody, has decided to venture out of her comfort zone, listen to her therapist, and ignore her compulsions. When a storm knocks out her power, a creature begins haunting her throughout the night, turning her terrifying thoughts into a realistic nightmare. Battling her OCD literally is now the path she'll take to escape. Her only haven is her phone, with whom she messages constantly throughout the night.
