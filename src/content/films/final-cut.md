By day, Ethan Dalton is a celebrated filmmaker admired for his cinematic precision and emotional depth. By night, he becomes consumed by a hidden obsession that blurs the line between his art and his darker impulses. As his double life unravels, the boundaries between creation and destruction collapse, forcing him to confront the monstrous reflection of his own genius.

A psychological thriller about obsession, artistry, and identity, Final Cut is a sharp meditation on fame and control, where Ethan Dalton's unraveling blurs the line between creation and madness until reality itself comes apart.
